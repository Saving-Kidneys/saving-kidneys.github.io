Kroger Community Rewards

Kroger is committed to helping our communities grow and prosper. Help us earn money by joining Kroger's free Community Rewards program.

1. Create a Kroger account or sign in to your existing account.

2. On the Community Rewards page, search "IP186" and select "Health & Awareness INC" and click Enroll.

3. Any transactions going forward using your free Shopper's Card (Plus Card) will help support our cause - at no added cost to you.

Health Education Workshops

For over 11 years, Health & Awareness, Inc has facilitated health workshops throughout Hampton Roads, Virginia. Our workshops focus on practical, actionable health education.

Topics include understanding lab results, reading nutritional labels, managing diabetes and high blood pressure, and our signature "Common Sense Rules" approach to better kidney care.
